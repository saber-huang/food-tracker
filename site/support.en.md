# Food Log App · Support

This app helps you keep track of what you eat. Snap a photo of a meal and an AI estimates its calories, protein, carbs and fat, or log it manually. It works out daily targets from your profile and can suggest recipes that fit them.

## FAQ

- **How accurate are the AI estimates?** Calories and nutrients are AI estimates from your photo, for reference only. You can edit each item before saving, or log it manually instead.
- **Where is my data stored?** Your profile and food log are stored only on your phone. For what's sent when you use AI features, see the [Privacy Policy](privacy-en.html).
- **How do I delete all my data?** On the Profile tab, tap Delete All Data. After you confirm, your profile, food log and settings on this phone are deleted. This can't be undone.
- **How do I withdraw consent for AI features?** On the Profile tab, tap Withdraw AI Consent. You'll be asked again before your next use of an AI feature.
- **How many times a day can I use AI features?** To control costs, photo scans and recipe generations are limited per device per day. The app shows how many you have left today.
- **Can I use US units?** Yes. On the Profile tab, set Units to US (ft, lb). Food weights always use grams.
- **Who is this app for?** Its formulas are based on research in adults, so it's intended only for adults 18 and older. Calorie and nutrition targets are not medical advice. If you have a health condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have a history of eating disorders, please talk to a doctor or registered dietitian first.
